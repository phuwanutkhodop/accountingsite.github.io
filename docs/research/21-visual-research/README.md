# Visual research for #21 — scarce, high-value design details (in progress)

**Why:** the owner rejected the #21 sample presets as not commercial grade (6 October 2026). The real presets must come
from what top professional work does that ordinary sites do not. See `../../decisions/21-section-library-sample.md` §8.

**Method:**
1. Sites were captured in a real browser (Chromium 141): desktop 1440 px and iPhone, frame by frame while scrolling,
   plus the techniques found in their CSS and scripts (`tools/capture.cjs`, list in `tools/sites.tsv`).
2. Reviewers looked at every frame and recorded only distinctive details (`tools/BRIEF.md`), each with a crop as evidence,
   rarity 1–5, fit for a professional firm 1–5, buildability and three-language fit.

**Groups reviewed so far:**
- finance, law and consulting (16 sites, 45 findings);
- luxury (8 sites, 21 findings);
- editorial and product craft (14 sites, 35 findings);
- Thai sites (13 findings + Thai typesetting rules);
- the **baseline**: paid accounting-firm templates, Big-4 Thailand, a Thai accounting firm and our own current site. It
  records what "ordinary" looks like (`baseline.md`).

**Still to review:** design studios (about 19 sites) and award winners (about 15 sites).

**Screenshots and crops are not in this repository:** they are other companies' work, kept for private review only.
Image links inside the `.md` files therefore do not resolve here.
