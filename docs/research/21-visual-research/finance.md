## Pattern across the group

Independently, Pictet (`pictet-2`), KKR (`kkr-2`) and Evercore (`ev-1`, `ev-2`) borrow **financial-statement conventions** for their numbers: a unit stated apart from the figure ('BN USD', '(VALUES IN MILLIONS)'), a dated *as at* footnote, an asterisk to the note, and honest qualifiers (~ for approximate, + for cumulative). Lazard (`lazard-1`), Slaughter and May (`sm-1`) and Wachtell (`wlrk-3`) do the same for work: a dated register of matters with 'Undisclosed' allowed. For an accounting firm this is the most natural and least imitable language available — it is how its own deliverables already look. Worth turning into a builder 'statement register' type system (figure · unit line · label · note · as-at date) rather than a stats block.

## Findings

### 1. Architectural line-drawing timeline (world events below the line, firm events above) — `pictet-1` (pictet.com)

![pictet-1](crops/pictet-1.jpg)

- **Rarity** 5 · **Fit** 5 · **r×f** 25 · **Role** proof
- **What:** Full-bleed pale-grey band (#f6f6f6, ~650px tall) that scrolls sideways. One 1px grey horizontal axis at ~70% of the band height; year labels (12px sans, lining figures) interrupt the axis with small gaps ('1803–1815 —— 1815 —— 1825 —— 1850'). Under each year, a 2–4 line caption (~11px, max ~150px wide) names a WORLD event (Napoleonic wars, Geneva joins the Confederation, US financial panic). ABOVE the axis, firm milestones rise as 1px grey vertical stems of different heights, each topped by a two-line label: year in small bold sans + the street address in tracked small caps ('3, COUR SAINT-PIERRE', '26, rue de la Cité'). At the foot of each stem, standing on the axis like a street elevation, is a monochrome line drawing of the building the firm occupied. One accent stem (2px oxblood, with a dot on the axis) marks the current scrub position and carries a live counter ('5 EMPLOYEES', tracked caps). The chapter text ('1805 / THE ORIGINS OF PICTET' + paragraph) is set in oxblood at the left edge.
- **Why valuable:** Turns 'since 1805' into checkable evidence: real addresses, headcount and the crises the firm lived through. The drawn buildings are a costly, unique asset, but the language is only hairlines and small type — archival, engineer-like, unhurried. Nobody can copy it because it is made of the firm's own facts.
- **Buildable:** needs-bespoke-assets — SVG line drawings of the firm's premises; axis, stems and labels are css (horizontal scroll-snap), the moving accent stem/counter is small-js. Without JS it degrades to a plain horizontally scrollable strip.
- **Three languages:** yes — labels are short and left-aligned; replace the tracked-caps address line with a lighter weight in Thai/Chinese.
- **Evidence:** `desktop-02.jpg` [0, 170, 1440, 490] → `crops/pictet-1.jpg`
