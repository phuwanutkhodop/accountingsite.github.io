Every image uses the locked palette tokens, so it recolours with day/night. A Colour / Duotone / B&W switch tests treatments on the image tabs. Initials "FN" and "EST. 2026" are placeholders until the firm's name and founding year are given.

**Technical notes (Claude):** all images are inline SVG (turbulence, lighting and blur filters; procedural guilloche curves), so they weigh a few KB, need no licence and can be generated by the builder at publish time. "Illustrations" stay banned (#33): the library is materials, light, letterforms and engraving, never drawn scenes or icons.

## 4. Round 2 — DA marks, redesigned from outside inspiration

The owner gave the firm's name and asked for a redesign drawing on outside inspiration. Inspiration notes: `research_notes/DA brand mark inspiration/inspiration.md` (search-summary evidence; brand sites were blocked by the proxy).

DA Brand Marks (private artifact): https://claude.ai/artifact/59ZFoNcbQYsCJs17igPxR2

- **M Monogram (8):** D holds A (Dürer's AD reversed; the A's crossbar touches both sides of the D) · in an oval (Cartier) · single line · crossbar becomes the frame (one-line system, Erik Penser Bank) · italic cipher · hallmark cartouche · verified total (accountant's double underline) · microtext ring
- **W Wordmark (6):** three tiers (Hermès "Paris" logic) · cipher + name · one line small caps · two lines left · on the oxblood bar · legal line with hallmark strip
- **S Seals (6):** brand seal (English top arc, Thai descriptor bottom arc) · banknote seal (guilloche + microtext) · oval hallmark · wax seal · bookplate · three-language descriptor ring
- **P Patterns (8):** microtext frame · double underline on totals · rosette behind the seal · saddle stitch · A-peak chevron (Goyard logic) · hallmark footer · engraved band with microtext · ledger watermark
- **X In real use (6):** letterhead · business card · website header · small sizes (96/48/32/16 px) · monthly report cover · LINE profile and email signature

**Rules adopted from the inspiration research (Claude):**

- The brand seal is deliberately **not** the registered company seal (which must carry the Thai registered name). No Garuda, crown or state emblem.
- **No Chinese name seal** until the firm has an approved Chinese name, and its characters must come from a calligrapher or licensed seal-script font, never generated. The round-1 诚信 seal is withdrawn.
- Patterns must encode something real (the firm's name in microtext, a checked total, a stitch); at most one guilloche element per page.
- The double rule doubles as the accountant's double underline under final totals.

Still needed from the owner: Thai registered name, Chinese name (if any), founding year, licence numbers (the "CPA" and "Licence No. 0000" in the mock-ups are placeholders).
